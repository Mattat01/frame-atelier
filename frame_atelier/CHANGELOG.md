# Changelog

## 1.0.0

Initial release.

- Unsplash search (infinite scroll, favourites, multi-select) and full-screen 4K upload.
- Library: thumbnails with disk cache, current-image hero, set-as-current, bulk delete.
- Per-image **mattes** and **photo filters** (changeable live, no re-upload).
- App-driven **slideshow** with interval + shuffle, and safety: skips while you're
  watching live TV, queues (doesn't interrupt) when you set art, and auto-pauses if
  the image is changed on the TV remote.
- Global **brightness** and **colour temperature** controls.
- In-app **Guide** tab.
- Settings and the TV pairing token persist in `/data`.
